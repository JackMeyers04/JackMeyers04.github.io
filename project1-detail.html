Thaumaturgy AI

GitHub Repository Live Demo
Role: AI Programmer Duration: 9/2025 - 5/2026 Software: UE5

Project Overview

Thaumaturgy is a factory building game where you play as a wizard banished underground. You must amass great power to emerge back to the surface. This project served as a vertical slice of our idea for a class assignment.

My Job

In this project, It was my job to develop the enemy and friendly AI. I created three enemy types: a Stalker (Earth), an Exploding enemy(Fire), and a Jumping enemy (Air). For the friendly AI, I designed a Mining Sprite that helps the player automate the mining process.
