Pathogen Survivors

Role: AI Programmer Duration: 6/2026 - Current Software: Godot

Project Overview

Pathogen Panic is a browser based 2d shooter with interactive learning experiences about pathogens, antibiotics, and vaccines

My Job

In this project, It was my job to develop the enemy AI and their functions such as the E-Coli enemy's pack hunting behavior, the Influenza enemy's infection behavior, and the Streptococcus enemy's debugging behaviors.

Key Features

  • EColi
    • Ecoli attack healthy cells together in a pack.
    • After Ecoli Feed enough they split multiplying into more Ecoli.
  • Streptococcus
    • Streptococcus cirlces around a healthy cell debugging it by making it take extra damage from EColi.
    • If A Streptococcus comes near another one it combines leveling it up increasing its debuff percentage.
  • Influenza
    • Influenza attacks the player when close enough it takes over the player for 3 seconds stopping it from attacking for that durration.
    • Influenza can only be killed by one specific antibiotic.
